# What is this Project ?
 this is an inventory and fleet management system for businesses to manage their warehouses, drivers, Stocks and The flow of their products
 via using this they can make sure all of their Stores have a constant supply of products, how much they owe each driver for their Product moving Trips
 who was the manager of warehouse #3 when for Event "x" happened and much more... simply put this was made so it would make the business owner have a
 easier time to see clear Data that shows the Flow of his product and be able Manage the whole business without needing to fill a 100 page note book
 every week
